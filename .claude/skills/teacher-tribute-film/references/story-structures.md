# Story Structures for Teacher Tribute Films

Each structure below gives beats as a **percentage of runtime**. Convert them to
seconds for the brief's duration. Example timings are listed for 60 s / 3 min / 7 min.
For beat vocabulary and alternative arcs, see
`cinematic-ui/references/data/narrative-beats.md` (Opening / Development / Resolution beats).

General rules:
- One protagonist point of view. For a tribute, that is usually a student or the teacher themselves.
- One concrete object that travels through the film (a chalk stub, a red pen, a notebook, a bicycle).
- The emotional peak sits at 75–85 % of runtime. Never put it in the last 5 s.
- The final 10 % is quiet: a dedication card, a breath, then the title.

---

## 1. Người lái đò — The Ferryman

The teacher carries generation after generation across the river and stays behind.

| Beat | % | 60 s | 3 min | 7 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Image of the teacher alone (dawn, empty class) | 0–10 | 0–6 | 0–18 | 0–42 |
| Students arrive, years pass (montage of cohorts) | 10–45 | 6–27 | 18–81 | 42–189 |
| One student struggles; the teacher's single act | 45–70 | 27–42 | 81–126 | 189–294 |
| Students leave ("crossing"); the teacher remains | 70–85 | 42–51 | 126–153 | 294–357 |
| Return: a former student comes back (20/11) | 85–95 | 51–57 | 153–171 | 357–399 |
| Dedication card | 95–100 | 57–60 | 171–180 | 399–420 |

Best modes: A, C. Motifs: river/boat (literal or as a visual metaphor), school drum.

## 2. Bây giờ & Ngày ấy — Then & Now

An adult alumnus revisits the old school. The present is intercut with memories.

| Beat | % |
|---|---|
| Present: adult at the school gate, hesitating | 0–10 |
| Trigger object/sound pulls into memory (match cut) | 10–20 |
| Memory: a small scene showing the teacher's character | 20–45 |
| Present: the place has changed (moving walk, details) | 45–55 |
| Memory: the turning point the student never thanked them for | 55–75 |
| Present: the meeting, or the discovery the teacher has retired/passed | 75–90 |
| Gesture instead of a speech; dedication | 90–100 |

Best modes: A, A+B. Grade memories warm and soft, the present natural.
Use match cuts on the object (the old notebook ↔ the new notebook).

## 3. Một ngày của cô — A Day in the Life

Observational, from dawn to night, with no narration until the end.

| Beat | % |
|---|---|
| Before dawn: lesson preparation, family | 0–15 |
| Commute (bicycle, motorbike, boat, mountain path) | 15–25 |
| Teaching: three short moments with three different students | 25–60 |
| After school: tutoring, home visits, grading at night | 60–85 |
| One line of VO or text: the number of years/students | 85–95 |
| Dedication | 95–100 |

Best modes: A, documentary style. Strong for highland/rural teachers.
Get consent and avoid poverty spectacle (see the cultural guide).

## 4. Lá thư — The Letters

Voice-overs of letters from several students across years. Visuals are archive or recreated.

| Beat | % |
|---|---|
| A handwritten envelope; the first voice (child) | 0–15 |
| Voice 2 (teenager): a memory of discipline | 15–35 |
| Voice 3 (university student): understanding it now | 35–60 |
| Voice 4 (adult/parent): the full realization | 60–85 |
| The teacher reads; we see only hands and a smile | 85–95 |
| Dedication | 95–100 |

Best modes: B, C. Very efficient for schools with old photos. Cast real students' voices with consent.

## 5. Chữ — The Handwriting (60 s or under, social-first)

A single continuous idea, suited to TikTok/Reels.

| Beat | % |
|---|---|
| Child's shaky handwriting, red-pen correction | 0–25 |
| Handwriting matures across quick match cuts (grades 1→12) | 25–70 |
| Adult signature on a document (diploma, contract, letter) | 70–85 |
| Text: "Mỗi nét chữ đều có một người thầy." | 85–100 |

Best modes: A (inserts only, no faces needed, easy consent), C. Vertical 9:16.

## Choosing

| Brief says | Recommend |
|---|---|
| Honor one specific teacher, alumni-made | 2 Then & Now, 4 Letters |
| Honor the whole staff at a school ceremony | 1 Ferryman, 4 Letters |
| Little budget, many old photos | 4 Letters |
| No consent for faces / social-first | 5 Handwriting |
| Highland/rural teacher, documentary feel | 3 A Day in the Life |
