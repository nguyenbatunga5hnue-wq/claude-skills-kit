# Cultural Guide — Ngày Nhà giáo Việt Nam (20/11)

## Context

- 20 November is Vietnamese Teachers' Day. Schools hold a ceremony (lễ kỷ niệm,
  văn nghệ), students visit teachers and bring flowers, alumni return to their old
  schools. Many tribute films screen at that ceremony, so the projector and hall
  audio are often the real screening conditions.
- The values the film usually draws on: *Tôn sư trọng đạo* (respect for teachers),
  *Uống nước nhớ nguồn* (remember the source when you drink the water),
  *Không thầy đố mày làm nên* (without a teacher you will not succeed).
  Use them as subtext; quoting one on screen is fine, quoting three is a cliché.

## Visual and sound motifs

| Motif | What it can mean | Sound pairing |
|---|---|---|
| Viên phấn, bụi phấn trên vai áo, tóc | Years of quiet work, ageing | Chalk scratching on the board |
| Bảng đen / bảng xanh, lau bảng | Starting over, erasing and rewriting | Board eraser taps |
| Bút mực đỏ, vở chấm điểm | Care, correction, late nights | Pen on paper, a ticking clock at night |
| Áo dài (cô giáo), áo sơ mi trắng | Dignity, tradition | — |
| Trống trường | Time, beginning and end of a school year | School drum (trống trường) |
| Con đò, dòng sông ("người lái đò") | Teacher ferries students across, then rows back alone | Water, oars |
| Hoa phượng (end of year), hoa cúc/hồng (20/11) | Farewell, gratitude | Cicadas (tiếng ve) for summer/farewell |
| Cửa sổ lớp học, ánh nắng xiên | Memory, nostalgia | Distant children's voices |
| Đôi dép mòn, chiếc xe đạp cũ | Sacrifice, rural teachers | Bicycle bell |
| Giáo án viết tay, đèn bàn | Preparation, devotion | Rain on a tin roof (mưa mái tôn) |

Pick 2–3 motifs and pay each off at least twice. A film that uses all of them is a montage of clichés.

## Clichés to avoid or subvert

- A teacher crying while reading a thank-you card as the only emotional beat.
- Generic stock footage of anonymous classrooms intercut with a pop song.
- The teacher as a flawless saint. Small human details (being tired, a joke,
  being strict for a reason) are more moving.
- Speeches that list virtues ("cô hiền, cô tận tụy, cô hy sinh..."). Show one act instead.
- An ending where an adult student "becomes a teacher too". It is valid but
  predictable, so earn it with a specific callback.

## Ethics, consent and sensitivity

- **Minors**: get written parental/school consent before showing identifiable
  students. Record this in the brief. Without consent, shoot hands, backs and silhouettes instead.
- **Real teachers**: get consent from the teachers being honored, especially for
  personal stories (illness, poverty, loss). Let them review the cut if their life is portrayed.
- **Gifts and envelopes**: avoid showing money or envelopes as gifts unless the
  film deliberately critiques it. The subject is sensitive in Vietnam.
- Avoid political slogans and party/state symbols unless the commissioning body requires them.
- Rural or mountain-area teachers (giáo viên vùng cao): portray their communities with
  dignity, not as poverty spectacle. Use ethnic minority language and dress accurately and with consent.

## Language and register

- Address forms carry the relationship: *thầy/cô – em* (in class), *thầy/cô – con*
  (in the South, or a close and long relationship), *thầy/cô – tôi/mình* (an adult alumnus narrating).
- Regional voice: choose a Northern, Central or Southern narrator to match the
  setting. Don't mix accents within one character.
- Subtitles: Vietnamese subtitles help sound-off social viewing. English
  subtitles help with diaspora audiences or international schools.

## Music rights

- Well-known teacher songs ("Bụi phấn", "Người thầy", "Mong ước kỷ niệm xưa",
  "Bông hồng tặng cô"...) are copyrighted. Showing them at a school-internal
  screening is usually tolerated, but online upload can be claimed, muted or blocked.
  Clear rights with VCPMC/the rights holder, use licensed stock music, or commission an original score.
- A student choir or band performing a song is still a cover. The composition rights still apply.
- Log every track's source and license in `05-sound-plan.md` and in the credits.
